# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are Malaysian SME owners and operators who need to source supplies, move surplus inventory, join pooled purchases, and understand the operational savings created by shared logistics. The current product also serves prospective members who browse the public marketplace before signing in.

## Product Purpose

FoodLink is a local circular-supply network. It helps businesses list usable surplus, discover nearby supply, combine demand into group buys, reserve items, and track estimated cost and logistics savings.

## Positioning

FoodLink connects procurement and surplus exchange in one workflow: nearby stock, buyer intent, group demand, supplier reliability, and delivery consolidation are presented as one operating system rather than separate classifieds and spreadsheet processes.

## Operating Context

Users work from phones and desktop browsers while checking stock, planning purchases, comparing nearby suppliers, creating listings, joining group buys, and reviewing account-linked transactions. Firebase Authentication supplies identity and Firestore stores profiles, listings, orders, and group buys.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Public visitors can browse marketplace listings and product explanations.
- Signed-in users can create listings, reserve live inventory, create group buys, customize a business profile, and review account activity.
- The application is a React/Vite single-page app deployed with Firebase Hosting and Firestore.
- Google sign-in is the current authentication method.
- Figures currently embedded as demo or estimated data must be presented honestly and must not be framed as externally verified claims.
- Firestore authorization must preserve listing ownership and order-participant integrity.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is FoodLink. Its voice is direct, practical, locally grounded, and optimistic about lower waste and better margins. Malaysian Ringgit, Klang Valley locations, local SME language, and the Waste2Wealth/circular-economy proposition are durable product cues.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the working React interface, Firebase configuration, Firestore rules, seeded marketplace examples, and a live Firebase Hosting URL. It contains no supplied photography, customer testimonials, audited impact study, pricing plan, or independently verified performance claims; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Make the next operational action obvious.
2. Show why a match is useful, not merely that an algorithm produced it.
3. Treat trust, ownership, and transaction integrity as product features.
4. Keep circular-economy impact connected to real marketplace activity.
5. Work comfortably on a busy operator's phone as well as desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, readable contrast, touch targets suitable for mobile operators, and language that remains clear for users with mixed technical fluency.
